## Data Risk Summary

MedDefense's most significant data protection weakness remains **patient data "in use" at the clinical endpoint layer** (unattended EHR sessions at nurse stations), but the newly confirmed network architecture reveals an equally serious weakness underneath it: because the entire Central network is flat with **no VLANs and no database-level access restriction** (`ehr-db-01` reachable from anywhere on the `/16`), the same Restricted-tier patient data is exposed in a second, structurally deeper way — not just visually to a passerby, but technically to any compromised device anywhere in the building, including the ~200 medical IoT devices and workstations that share that network. These two gaps compound rather than substitute for each other: even if the nurse-station session issue were fixed tomorrow, the database's network-wide reachability would remain, and vice versa.
